Simple Spaghetti

Description

This classic spaghetti recipe is quick to prepare and perfect for weeknights.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz spaghetti
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Sauté garlic in a pan, add tomato sauce, and simmer.
  3. Toss cooked spaghetti with sauce and season.
